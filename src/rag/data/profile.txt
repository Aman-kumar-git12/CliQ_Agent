# Feature: Profile
Route: /profile, /public-profile/:userId

## Summary
Your profile shows your photo, name, email, post count, network count and four tabs: Posts, Connections, Groups and Expertise. Other people's profiles have the same tabs plus buttons to connect and chat.

## How to open your profile
1. Click **Profile** in the left sidebar, or tap **Me** in the bottom bar on a phone.
- The stat cards are **Posts**, **Network** and **Groups**.
- The tabs are **Posts**, **Connections**, **Groups** and **Expertise**.

## How to change your profile photo
1. Open your profile.
2. Click the camera icon on your profile picture.
3. Choose **Upload Image** and pick an image file.
4. In the "Update Profile Photo" dialog click **Update**.
- Choose **View Image** from the same menu to see your current photo full size.
- You can also paste an image link into **Profile Image URL** on the Edit Profile page (Settings > **Edit Profile**).

## How to edit your name, email or age
1. Open **Settings** and click **Edit Profile**.
2. Change the fields and click **Save Changes**, then **Yes, Update**.
- The profile page itself has no edit button.

## Profile tabs
- **Posts**: a grid of your posts ("My Posts"). Reposts carry a **Repost** badge. Click a tile to open the post.
- **Connections**: your accepted connections. Each has a **Message** button that opens a chat.
- **Groups**: group chats you belong to. Creating groups is not available yet.
- **Expertise** ("My Expertise"): your expertise profile in the layout you chose. The eye icon shows it full screen. To edit it, use Settings > **Edit Expertise**.

## Other people's profiles
1. Click someone's name or photo anywhere in CliQ.
2. Use **Connect** to send a connection request (it changes to **Requested**, or shows **Connected** once accepted).
3. Use **Chat** to open a conversation with them.
- Their tabs are the same: **Posts** ("Recent Posts"), **Connections**, **Groups** and **Expertise** ("Professional Stack").

## Hover cards
- Holding the mouse over a person's photo for a moment shows a card with their posts, connections and groups counts, a few recent posts, and a **Connect** button (**Request Sent** after clicking, **Connected** if already connected).

## Limits
- There is no username. Handles like @firstname_lastname are made from your name and cannot be edited.
- Your email is shown on your profile and on your public profile.
- Not available yet: changing the cover banner, removing or cropping your photo, private profiles, blocking or reporting a person, removing a connection from the profile.

## Common problems
- Problem: "Failed to upload image" → Fix: choose a normal image file and try again.
- Problem: blank fields on your expertise show a row of asterisks → Fix: fill those fields in Settings > **Edit Expertise** and click **Save Expertise**.
- Problem: a Connect button shows the wrong state on someone's profile → Fix: check **Requests** or your **Connections** tab; the button state can be out of date.
