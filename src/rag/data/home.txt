# Feature: Home

## Overview
The Home page shows a random public post feed and a sidebar with suggested users.

## What users can do
- Scroll through posts loaded from the random feed endpoint.
- See post content, images, like counts, comment counts, and basic author details.
- Like posts and open comments from each post card.
- Open user profiles from feed content.
- Use the right sidebar to view suggested users.
- Refresh suggested users from the sidebar.
- Open the built-in CliQ AI chat assistant from Home.

## Feed behavior
- The feed is not based on followed connections.
- The backend returns random posts from other users and excludes the logged-in user's own posts.
- Infinite scrolling loads more random posts while avoiding duplicates already shown in the current session.

## Suggested content
- The sidebar shows suggested users from the backend suggestion endpoint.
- The AI assistant can also help by suggesting users and posts when asked.

## Limits
- Home does not provide multi-account switching.
- The feed is not described by the code as a real-time synchronized stream.
