# Feature: Posts and interactions
Route: /post/:postId, /post/:postId/edit, /saved

## Summary
On any post you can like, comment, reply to comments, repost or quote, save, share and report. Only the author can edit or delete a post, from the post's own page. Saved posts are on the Saved page.

## How to like a post
1. Click the heart under the post. Click it again to unlike.
- Hover over the heart to see "Liked by".
- The author gets a notification. Liking comments is not available yet.

## How to comment and reply
1. Click the comment icon under a post. The post opens with the comment box.
2. Type in **Post your reply · @ to mention** and click **Reply**.
3. To answer a comment, click **Reply** under it, type, and click **Reply** again (or **Cancel**).
- Replies to replies go into the same thread and start with an @mention of that person.
- Use "View 2 replies" (the number varies) / **Hide replies** to open or close a thread.
- Comments can be up to 1,000 characters.

## How to delete or report a comment
1. Click the three-dot button on the comment.
2. On your own comment choose **Delete comment** (or **Delete reply**), then **Delete** to confirm. Deleting a comment also deletes its replies.
3. On someone else's comment choose **Report**.
- You can only delete your own comments. Editing comments is not available yet.

## How to repost or quote
1. Click the repost icon (two arrows) under a post.
2. Choose **Repost** to share it as is, or **Quote** to add your own text.
3. For a quote, write in "Add your thoughts…" (up to 500 characters) and click **Post**.
- Click **Undo repost** in the same menu to remove a repost. A quote is a normal post; delete it to remove it.
- The author gets a notification.

## How to save and find saved posts
1. Click the bookmark icon on a post, or choose **Save post** in its three-dot menu. Click again (**Remove from saved**) to unsave.
2. Open saved posts with **Saved** in the left sidebar (on a phone: menu at the top right > **Saved**).
- Saves are private. Nobody is notified.

## How to share a post
1. Click **Share** under the post, or **Copy link** in its three-dot menu.
- On phones the share sheet opens; otherwise the link is copied ("Link copied to clipboard").

## How to report a post
1. Click the three-dot menu on the post and choose **Report post** (on the post page: **Report Post**).
2. Choose a reason: Spam, Hate or Abuse, Harassment, Threats or Violence, Sexual or Explicit, Offensive Language, Bullying, Impersonation, or Other.
3. Click **Submit Report**.
- Reports are anonymous. You can report a post or comment once; the option then shows **Reported**.

## How to edit your post
1. Open the post (click its text).
2. At the top right click the pencil icon (**Edit Post**).
3. Change the text (up to 500 characters) and click **Save Changes**. Click **Cancel** to stop.
- Only the text can be edited. You cannot add, change or remove the photo or video.
- The text cannot be left empty.

## How to delete your post
1. Open the post (click its text).
2. At the top right click the trash icon (**Delete Post**).
3. In "Delete Post?" click **Delete Post**.
- This cannot be undone. Plain reposts of it are removed; quotes of it show "This post is no longer available."

## Limits
- Only the author can edit or delete a post. Edit and delete are on the post page, not in the feed card menu.
- Not available yet: comment likes, editing comments, deleting other people's comments on your post, pinning comments, saved-post folders.

## Common problems
- Problem: "Failed to update post." → Fix: the text can't be empty and must be 500 characters or fewer; only the author can edit.
- Problem: "Failed to delete post." → Fix: only the author can delete a post; try again.
- Problem: "Failed to report ❌" → Fix: you may have reported it already.
- Problem: "Couldn't repost" → Fix: try again; the original may have been deleted.
