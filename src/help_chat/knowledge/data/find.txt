# Feature: Search
Route: /search

## Summary
Search is the one place to find people, posts and hashtags. With an empty search box it shows **Suggested Network**, people you are not connected with yet. People search understands skills and roles, and longer descriptions use AI meaning-based matching. (Find People used to be a tab on Explore; it is now part of Search.)

## How to search for people, posts and hashtags
1. Click **Search** in the left sidebar, or the search icon at the top on a phone. The **Explore** link in the Home sidebar's Discover People box also opens Search.
2. Type in the box (e.g. "React", "#ai" or "people who know React and ML"). Results appear as you type.
3. Use the tabs **All**, **People**, **Posts** and **Hashtags**.
- Click a hashtag to see posts with that tag.
- People found by AI meaning carry an **AI match** badge, and each card shows what matched, e.g. "Skills: React".

## How to connect with someone from Search
1. Find the person in **Suggested Network** (empty search box) or in the **People** results.
2. Click a name to open the profile, or click **Connect** to send a request.
- The button shows **Requested** after you send a request and **Connected** once accepted.
- Switch between list and grid view with the two icons next to the section title.

## What people search matches
- First and last name (start of the name).
- Expertise fields: skills, role/title, interests, experience, headline, about you and projects.
- Searches of two or more words (e.g. "backend developer with cloud experience") also use AI meaning-based matching, so people with related skills appear even without the exact words.
- Single words and #hashtags use normal keyword search.

## Suggested users
- **Suggested Network** on Search (5 people) and **Discover People** on Home (3 people) are random people you have no connection request with.
- These suggestions are not AI-matched. AI-matched suggestions are on **Explore** (Get Connections).

## Limits
- Searches use up to 100 characters. Search shows 8 results per section on **All** and 20 on a single tab.
- You and blocked users never appear in people results.
- Not available yet: search filters (location, date), search history, searching inside messages.

## Common problems
- Problem: "AI matching is unavailable right now. Showing keyword matches only." → Fix: results still work by keywords; try again later for AI matches.
- Problem: no results → Fix: try a shorter query, a skill name, or the person's first name.
- Problem: **Connect** shows again for someone who rejected you, and clicking it gives an error → Fix: a rejected request cannot be resent.
- Problem: an old link to Find People → Fix: it now opens Search automatically.
