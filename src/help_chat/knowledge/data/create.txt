# Feature: Create a post
Route: /create/post

## Summary
You can publish a post with text, one image, or one video. Post text can be up to 500 characters, and you can mention people with @ and add topics with #. Posts are public.

## How to create a post with a photo or video
1. Click **New Post** in the left sidebar (or **Create** in the bottom bar on a phone).
2. Write your text in **Description** ("What's on your mind? Use @ to mention, # for topics"). The counter shows how many of the 500 characters you've used.
3. Optionally click **Upload Media** and choose one image or one video.
4. Click **Share Post**.
5. In the "Create this post?" dialog click **Post**.
6. You see "Post uploaded successfully" and are taken to your profile.
- Remove the chosen file with the X on its preview. Click **Cancel** to leave without posting.

## Post text limit
- The maximum post length is 500 characters.
- A post needs text, an image or a video (at least one of them).

## Photos and videos
- Yes, you can upload a video in a post.
- Each post can have one file: either one image or one video.
- Images must be under 10 MB. Videos must be under 50 MB.
- Only image and video files are accepted.

## Mentions, hashtags and links
- Type @ and a name to get suggestions; pick a person to mention them. They get a notification.
- Type # followed by a word to add a topic. Hashtags become links to search results.
- If the post has a link and no photo or video, a preview card (title, description, image) is shown.

## Limits
- Not available yet: drafts, scheduled posts, several images in one post, polls, private or connections-only posts.

## Common problems
- Problem: "Image should be less than 10 MB" or "Video should be less than 50 MB" → Fix: choose a smaller file.
- Problem: "Only image and video files are supported" → Fix: choose an image or video file.
- Problem: "Add some text or select media before posting" → Fix: write something or add a file.
- Problem: "Failed to create post" → Fix: check your connection and try again.
- Problem: the counter stops at 500 → Fix: shorten your text; 500 characters is the maximum.
