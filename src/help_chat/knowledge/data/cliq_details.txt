# Feature: About CliQ and CliQ AI
Route: /home (help chat button)

## Summary
CliQ is a professional networking web app. You build a profile and expertise, post updates, find and connect with people (including AI-matched suggestions), and chat one to one. CliQ AI is the help assistant that explains how to use CliQ.

## What you can do on CliQ
- Create an account with email or Google, and keep a profile with an expertise page.
- Post text, one image or one video; like, comment, reply, repost, quote, save, share and report posts.
- Search people, posts and hashtags.
- Get AI-matched people on Get Connections (**Explore**) and send connection requests.
- Chat one to one with text, images, files and voice messages, with AI reply suggestions.
- Get notifications for likes, comments, replies, mentions, reposts and connection requests.

## Main pages
- **Feed** (/home), **Search** (/search), **Explore** (/find/getconnection), **Requests** (/requests), **Messages** (/messages), **Notifications** (/notifications) in the sidebar's Feed section.
- **New Post** (/create/post) in the Create section.
- **Profile** (/profile), **Saved** (/saved) and **Settings** (/settings) in the You section.
- On a phone the bottom bar has **Feed**, **Explore**, **Create**, **Requests** and **Me**; search, notifications, messages and the menu (**Settings**, **Saved**, **Log out**) are at the top.

## What CliQ AI (this help assistant) can do
- Explain CliQ features, where to find them and how to use them, step by step.
- Answer questions about limits, errors and features that are not available yet.

## What CliQ AI cannot do
- It cannot do things for you (post, connect, change settings), and it cannot see your account, posts or messages.
- It cannot find or recommend specific people or posts. For that, use **Search** or **Explore** (Get Connections).
- It only answers questions about CliQ.

## Other AI features in CliQ
- AI reply suggestions and Ask AI inside chats, the Magic Builder for your expertise profile, AI matching on Get Connections, and AI meaning-based people search. See the AI features guide.

## Limits
- The help chat is available on the Home page only (round sparkles button, bottom right).
