# Feature: Home feed
Route: /home

## Summary
Home is the main feed. It shows posts from other people in random order, with a quick post box at the top and, on wide screens, a sidebar with Discover People. The CliQ AI help chat is opened from the round button at the bottom right of Home.

## What the feed shows
- Posts from other people, in random order. More load as you scroll; "✨ End of the universe ✨" means there are no more.
- Your own posts are not shown in your Home feed. They are on your profile (**Profile** > **Posts**).
- Reposts and quotes by other people appear too.
- On each post you can like, comment, repost, save and share (see the Posts and interactions guide).

## How to post from Home
1. Type in the box at the top ("What's on your mind, …?").
2. Optionally click **Mood** to add emojis.
3. Click **Post Now**.
- This box is for text only (up to 500 characters). To add a photo or video, use **New Post** in the sidebar.
- Your new post won't appear in your own feed; check your profile.

## How to open the CliQ AI help chat
1. On Home, click the round sparkles button at the bottom right.
2. Type in **Ask me anything...** and send.
- Use the menu icon for **Chat History**: **New Chat**, delete a chat, or **Clear All History**.

## Discover People
- On wide screens the right sidebar's **Discover People** shows 3 random people you are not connected with. **Explore** opens Search, where you can find people.
- These are not AI-matched; AI matches are on **Explore** (Get Connections).

## Limits
- Only the **For You** tab works. **Following** and **Trending** are not available yet.
- The Stories bar at the top is a preview only; stories are not available yet.
- The **Trending** list in the sidebar is sample content, and its **See all** link does not work yet.
- The **Follow** button in Discover People and the sidebar footer links (About, Help, Privacy, Terms…) do not work yet.

## Common problems
- Problem: "Failed to load feed" → Fix: refresh the page.
- Problem: you posted but can't see your post on Home → Fix: your own posts are only on your profile.
- Problem: you can't find the AI help chat → Fix: it is only on the Home page, bottom right.
