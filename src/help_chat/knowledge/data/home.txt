# Feature: Home feed
Route: /home

## Summary
Home is the main feed, with a quick post box at the top and three tabs: **For You**, **Following** and **Trending**. On wide screens a sidebar shows trending hashtags and Discover People. The CliQ AI help chat is opened from the round button at the bottom right.

## What the feed tabs show
- **For You**: posts from other people, in random order. More load as you scroll; "✨ End of the universe ✨" means there are no more.
- **Following**: the newest posts from your connections (people whose request you accepted or who accepted yours). Empty until you have connections who post.
- **Trending**: posts from the last 30 days, most liked first.
- Switching tabs keeps each tab's posts, so you don't lose your place.
- Your own posts are not shown in your Home feed. They are on your profile (**Profile** > **Posts**).
- Reposts and quotes by other people appear too.
- On each post you can like, comment, repost, save and share (see the Posts and interactions guide).

## How to post from Home
1. Type in the box at the top ("What's on your mind, …?").
2. Optionally click **Mood** to add emojis.
3. Click **Post Now**.
- This box is for text only (up to 500 characters). To add a photo or video, use **New Post** in the sidebar.
- Your new post won't appear in your own feed; check your profile.

## How to open the CliQ AI help chat
1. Click the round sparkles button at the bottom right. It is on every page except Messages.
2. Type in **Ask me anything...** and send.
- Use the menu icon for **Chat History**: **New Chat**, delete a chat, or **Clear All History**.

## Trending hashtags
- On wide screens the right sidebar's **Trending** list shows the most-used hashtags of the last 30 days, with how many posts use each.
- Click a hashtag to see its posts in Search; **See all** opens hashtag search.

## Discover People
- On wide screens the right sidebar's **Discover People** shows 3 random people you are not connected with. Click a name to open their profile, or **Connect** to send a request (the button then shows **Requested**).
- **Explore** next to the title opens Search, where you can find more people.
- These are not AI-matched; AI matches are on **Explore** (Get Connections).

## Limits
- Stories are not available.
- **Following** only includes accepted connections, not people with a pending request.

## Common problems
- Problem: "Failed to load feed" → Fix: refresh the page.
- Problem: you posted but can't see your post on Home → Fix: your own posts are only on your profile.
- Problem: you can't find the AI help chat → Fix: it is the round button at the bottom right of every page except Messages.
- Problem: the **Following** tab is empty → Fix: it shows posts from your connections only; connect with people on Search or Explore.
