# Feature: Messages
Route: /messages, /messages/:userId

## Summary
Messages is CliQ's one-to-one chat. You can send text, images, files and voice messages, reply to a message, edit your own messages and delete messages for yourself or for everyone. Messages arrive in real time.

## How to start a chat
1. Click **Messages** in the left sidebar (or the message icon at the top on a phone).
2. The inbox (**MESSAGES**) lists your chats and your accepted connections. Connections you haven't chatted with show "Start a new chat".
3. Click a person to open the chat.
- You can also click **Chat** on someone's profile, or **Message** in your profile's Connections tab.
- In an empty chat, tap the sticker ("Tap the sticker to start the conversation") to send a quick hello.
- Use **Search CLIQ...** to filter the inbox by name.

## How to send messages, photos, files and voice notes
1. Type in **Type a message...** and press Enter (Shift+Enter adds a new line), or click the send icon.
2. For a photo or file, click **+**, pick the file, optionally type in **Add a caption...**, then click the send icon.
3. For a voice message, click the microphone icon, speak, then click the send icon. Click **Cancel** to discard it.
- Use the smiley icon for emojis.
- Accepted files: images, .pdf, .doc, .docx and .txt.

## How to reply, copy, edit or delete a message
1. Click a message (or its small arrow) to open its menu.
2. Choose **Reply**, **Copy Text**, **Edit Message** (your own messages) or **Delete**.
- **Reply** shows "Replying to …" above the box. Replies work for text messages.
- **Edit Message** puts the text back in the box; change it and press Enter.
- **Delete** lets you select one or more messages, then click the trash icon. In "Delete Message?" choose **Delete for everyone** (only your own messages) or **Delete for me**.
- A message deleted for everyone shows "Message deleted".

## Online status
- The chat header shows **Active Now** when the person is online, otherwise **Offline**.

## Limits
- The inbox lists accepted connections, but you can also message anyone from the **Chat** button on their profile.
- Blocked accounts cannot use chat.
- Inbox search matches names only, not message text.
- Not available yet: group chats, voice or video calls, read receipts, typing indicators, unread counts in the inbox, searching inside a chat, forwarding, reactions, deleting a whole conversation, "edited" labels.

## Common problems
- Problem: "❌ Upload failed." or "Cloud upload failed" → Fix: check your connection and try a smaller file.
- Problem: "Permission denied for microphone" → Fix: allow microphone access for CliQ in your browser settings.
- Problem: a person isn't in your inbox → Fix: only accepted connections and past chats are listed; open their profile and click **Chat**.
- Problem: "Failed to delete messages" → Fix: try again; **Delete for everyone** only works for your own messages.
