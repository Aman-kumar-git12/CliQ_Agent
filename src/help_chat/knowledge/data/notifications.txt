# Feature: Notifications
Route: /notifications

## Summary
Notifications tell you when someone interacts with you: likes, comments, replies, mentions, reposts, connection requests and accepted requests. New ones arrive instantly and an unread count shows next to the bell or the Notifications menu item.

## How to see your notifications
1. On a computer click **Notifications** in the left sidebar. The badge shows the unread count.
2. On a phone tap the bell icon at the top.
- The page shows "N unread" or "You're all caught up".
- Click **Load more** for older ones.

## What you get notified about
- Someone likes your post ("liked your post"). Unliking removes it.
- Someone comments on your post ("commented: …").
- Someone replies to your comment ("replied: …").
- Someone mentions you with @ in a post, quote, comment or reply ("mentioned you…").
- Someone reposts or quotes your post ("reposted your post").
- Someone sends you a connection request ("wants to connect with you"). It disappears once you accept or reject it.
- Someone accepts your request ("accepted your connection request").
- You are never notified about your own actions.

## How to mark notifications as read
1. Click a notification to open it; it is marked read.
2. Click **Mark all read** to mark everything read.
- Click the X on a notification to dismiss it. This deletes it permanently.

## Where a notification takes you
- Likes, comments, replies, mentions and reposts open the post.
- Connection requests open **Requests**.
- Accepted requests open that person's profile.

## Limits
- Not available yet: notification settings or muting, email or push notifications, notifications for new messages, an unread-only filter, marking as unread.

## Common problems
- Problem: "No notifications yet" → Fix: likes, comments and connection requests will show up here once people interact with you.
- Problem: the unread count looks wrong → Fix: refresh the page.
- Problem: you don't get notified about messages → Fix: message notifications are not available yet; check **Messages**.
